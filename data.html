Smart Management
✦

Smart Management is an academic project developed by Arsath as part of the B.Sc. Information Technology program at Rathinam Global Deemed to be University.

The project focuses on using technology to make management processes more organized, efficient and user-friendly.

Smart Management demonstrates how digital systems can help users manage information, simplify everyday tasks and improve overall productivity.
